Camp Whaaat!? Details

What We Have For You:

  • World class fabrication and performance technology spaces of the ATLAS Institute and CU Boulder
  • A host of brilliant, skilled weirdos (and motivated creative technology students)
  • An established festival with a playful, curious audience to show your work to
  • Daily coffee and breakfast burritos
  • Camp Whaaat!? themed swag
  • An absolutely picturesque place to hang out alongside the mountains

What We're Asking of You:

  • Get Here (fly to DEN, we can pick you up from the airport)
  • Find a place to stay (in Boulder, we can help coordinate rooms and possibly get hotel discounts through the university)
  • Join daily camp meetups, and go on at least some planned excursions
  • Show a piece of work at the Whaaat!? Festival (can be something you already want to share, but ideally something you worked on during the week of camp)
  • Be Awesome, generally
